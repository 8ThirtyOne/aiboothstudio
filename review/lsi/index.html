About AI Booth Studio

AI Booth Studio is a new web app from 8ThirtyOne that helps companies visualize their trade show presence before anything is built. You describe a booth in a sentence or start from a standard size, shape it in 3D, and add your brand. AI then turns the design into photo-realistic images and short videos for investor decks, board presentations and internal sign-off.

It’s a concept-visualization tool for leaders and their teams, not a fabrication or CAD tool. Exhibit houses still build the real booth.

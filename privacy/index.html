This policy explains what information AI Booth Design, operated by [company legal name], collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, used to sign you in and link your pass.
  • Purchases: which pass you bought, the amount and the date. Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach our servers.
  • Designs and uploads: booth designs, logos, artwork and reference photos you add, plus the AI results you create.
  • Messages: what you send through the contact form.
  • Basic usage data: technical logs such as errors and page requests, used to keep the Service working.

How we use it

To provide the Service, process payments, send sign-in links, receipts and service emails, answer questions, prevent abuse and improve the product. We don't sell your personal information.

Services we rely on

  • Stripe: payments.
  • Supabase: accounts and the database.
  • Vercel: website and app hosting.
  • Resend: email delivery.
  • Anthropic (Claude): AI design features. Prompts and images you send to AI features are processed to produce your results.
  • AI image and video providers: to create photo-realistic results from your renders.

Storage and retention

Designs you don't save to your account stay in your browser. We keep account and purchase records while your account is active and as required for tax and accounting.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or close your account, through the contact form. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights, which we'll honor.

Cookies

We use only what's needed to keep you signed in and the site working. We don't use advertising cookies.

We want you to be happy with your pass. Here's how refunds work.

Full refund within 48 hours

If you're not satisfied, ask for a refund within 48 hours of purchase. We'll refund the full amount if you haven't used any AI images or AI videos from your pass.

After using AI images or video

AI images and videos cost us to create, so once any have been generated, passes aren't refundable, except where the law requires otherwise or something went wrong on our side.

If something went wrong

If you were charged twice, couldn't access your pass, or a feature failed and we couldn't fix it, contact us and we'll make it right with a refund or extra time.

How to ask

Use the contact form with the email you used at checkout. Approved refunds go back to the original card through Stripe, usually within 5–10 business days, and the pass ends when the refund is issued.

Refund Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Draft for review. This page is a starting point, not legal advice. Have it reviewed before launch, and fill in the highlighted details.

We want you to be happy with your pass. Here's how refunds work.

Full refund within 48 hours, only if no AI has been used

If you're not satisfied, ask for a refund within 48 hours of purchase. We'll refund the full amount only if none of your pass's AI features have been used, meaning:

  • No AI Build actions: no AI Build from a description, no photo to 3D, no AI prompt writing.
  • No AI Render images or videos: nothing generated, including ones that were then animated.

Designing, branding and exporting in the 3D studio without AI doesn't count as use.

After any AI has been used

Every AI Build action and every AI image or video costs us to create. Once any have been used, passes aren't refundable, except where the law requires otherwise or something went wrong on our side.

Extras

Extra AI Build actions, images and videos can be refunded within 48 hours only if none of them have been used.

If something went wrong

If you were charged twice, couldn't access your pass, or a feature failed and we couldn't fix it, contact us and we'll make it right with a refund or extra time.

How to ask

Use the contact form or email support@aiboothstudio.com from the email you used at checkout. Approved refunds go back to the original card through Stripe, usually within 5–10 business days, and the pass ends when the refund is issued.

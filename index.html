Step 1 · Describe it

Start with a sentence, or a photo.

Tell AI what you have in mind. It picks the size, booth type, walls, flooring, colors and furniture, and builds it in 3D in seconds.

  1. Click AI Build at the top of the studio.
  2. Describe the booth in a sentence, or add a photo of one you like.
  3. Click Build my booth. The design is ready to edit.

Prefer to start from scratch? Pick a size and booth type instead.

02
Step 2 · Shape it in 3D

Click, drag and adjust anything.

Fine-tune the layout like moving furniture in a room. Every piece has real-world sizes, and you can walk the aisle at eye level to see it the way visitors will.

  1. Choose the booth type (inline, corner, peninsula or island) and a size.
  2. Click any item to select it, then drag it across the floor. Double-click to rotate.
  3. Add counters, screens, seating, plants and signs from the 53-item library.

Step 3 · Brand it

Make it unmistakably yours.

Your logo, colors, fonts and artwork go straight onto the walls, counters and signs, so the booth looks like your company, not a template.

  1. Open the Brand tab and upload your logo and artwork.
  2. Set your company name, tagline and brand colors.
  3. Pick materials and lighting to match the mood of your show.
04
Step 4 · Render it

Frame the shot and save a 4K render.

Orbit around the booth, step into the aisle at eye level, and save a crisp render from any angle. These were rendered straight out of the app without retouching. Select one to open that design and make it your own.

  1. Pick a view: 3D, front, side, plan or eye level.
  2. Turn on lights and visitors for a show-day look.
  3. Click Export Render to save a 4K image.

Step 5 · Make it real

From render to the real thing.

AI Render turns your design into an ultra-realistic photo or 10-second video clip of the booth on a real show floor. Drop it into investor decks, board presentations and pitches, win sign-off on your concept, and promote your booth before the doors open.

  1. Frame the view you want in the 3D editor.
  2. Click AI Render and choose a style, or write your own art direction.
  3. Generate an image, then Animate it into a 10-second video.

FAQ

Questions exhibitors ask.

Do I need to install anything?

No. AI Booth Studio runs in your web browser on a laptop or desktop. A large desktop or laptop screen is highly recommended. On a phone or small tablet, the 3D view and controls are too small to design comfortably.

How do the AI features work?

AI is built in and included with every pass: AI build, photo-to-3D, and photo-realistic images and video. Each pass includes a set number of AI actions and image and video credits. Nothing extra to sign up for.

How do I get the ultra-realistic image or video?

Once your booth is designed, click Generate. AI uses your render and your art direction to create a photo-realistic image or a 10-second video clip of the finished booth, right in the app. Each pass includes credits: 4 images and 2 videos for a week, 8 images and 4 videos for a month, or 30 images and 10 videos for a year. Need more? Buy extra images for $5 each or videos for $10 each from the AI Render window. They’re added to your pass and can be used until it ends.

Do I need design or 3D experience?

No. You can start from a sentence or a photo, and change anything by clicking it. Every step is guided, from the first layout to the final photo-realistic visual.

Is this a CAD or fabrication tool?

No. AI Booth Studio is for concept visualization: showing what your trade show presence could look like, for investors, presentations, pitches and internal sign-off. It doesn't produce CAD drawings, engineering or manufacturing details. When you're ready to build, share your visuals with an exhibit house as a starting point. You can also give them a 4K render, a GLB 3D model and a summary of approximate sizes, and they will produce the construction drawings.

Do I need an account?

Yes, once you buy a pass. Sign in with the email you used at checkout and we'll send you a one-time sign-in link, so there's no password to remember. Your account holds your pass, your remaining image and video credits, and your generated images and videos.

Where are my designs saved?

When you're signed in, your designs save to your account automatically, so you can open them on any computer. You can also send a view-only share link to a colleague or investor.
